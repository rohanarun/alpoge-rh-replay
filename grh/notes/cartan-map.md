# Higman's criterion and multiplicative functions (the Cartan map)

This note explains
[`New.DiscreteModule.IsMultiplicative.eq_one_of_eq_one_on_isHigmanProjective`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L574),
the tool that lets the readable edition reduce statements about finite modules to projective ones.
The file header calls it a form of the known result that the Cartan map has finite cokernel
(Serre, *Linear Representations of Finite Groups*, Theorem 35). The proof uses no Brauer characters
or Artin induction; it uses functions on two parallel affine hyperplanes. Names below omit the
prefix `New.DiscreteModule.`.

## The setting

`G` is a compact group, `U` an open normal subgroup, `l` a prime, and `z ∈ G` is central modulo `U`
(every commutator `⁅z, g⁆` lies in `U`). The modules considered satisfy
[`FiniteKilledFixedInj l U z 1`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L80): finite, killed by
`l`, fixed by `U`, and `ρ(z) − 1` injective.

- [`IsHigmanProjective U`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L63) is Higman's criterion:
  some additive `τ` has `∑ g τ g⁻¹ = 1`, summed over representatives of `G ⧸ U`. It holds whenever
  the module is killed by an integer prime to the index
  ([`isHigmanProjective_of_coprime_index`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L129): take `τ`
  a Bézout multiple of the identity).
- A function `φ` with values in `ℚ≥0` on these modules is
  [`IsMultiplicative`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L462) if `φ B = φ A · φ D` for
  every short exact sequence `0 → A → B → D → 0` with `B` of this kind.

## The statement

If `φ` is multiplicative and equals `1` on every such module with Higman's property, then `φ = 1`
on every such module.

## The proof

1. **A central subgroup.**
   [`exists_normal_pow_mem_not_dvd_card`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L230) gives a
   normal subgroup `S` of `G ⧸ U` of order prime to `l` that contains the image of a power
   `z ^ l ^ j`: write the order of `z` as `l ^ j · n` with `l ∤ n`, and let `S` be generated by
   `z ^ l ^ j`, which is central. Put `Δ = (G ⧸ U) ⧸ S`.
2. **Function modules.** For a finite `Δ`-set `Y`, the module of functions `Y → A` with the diagonal
   action ([`diagonalPermModule`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L220)) is again of the
   kind considered ([`FiniteKilledFixedInj.diagonalPermModule`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L251)).
   A function fixed by `z` is fixed by `z ^ l ^ j`, which acts trivially on `Y`. And
   `z ^ l ^ j − 1 = (z − 1) ^ l ^ j` on a module killed by `l`. Put `f Y = φ(functions Y → A)`. Then
   `f` is `1` on empty sets. Splitting `Y` into an orbit and its complement gives a short exact
   sequence ([`exists_isShortExact_permModule`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L368)), so
   `f Y = f(Y ∖ orbit) · f(Δ ⧸ Stab y)`. Also `A` itself is the functions on the point `Δ ⧸ Δ`.
3. **A combinatorial lemma.**
   [`eq_one_of_prod_quotient`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L324) concerns such an `f`
   and a `Δ`-set `X` with a fixed point. If `f(X × Δ ⧸ K) = 1` for every subgroup `K`, then
   `f(Δ ⧸ K) = 1` for every `K`. The proof is an induction on `K`. Every set over `Δ ⧸ K` has `f` in
   the powers of `f(Δ ⧸ K)`, because its orbits are `Δ ⧸ K'` with `K' ≤ K`. The orbit of
   `(x, 1)` in `X × Δ ⧸ K` is a copy of `Δ ⧸ K`. So `1 = f(Δ ⧸ K) ^ (n + 1)` for some `n`, and in
   `ℚ≥0` this forces `f(Δ ⧸ K) = 1`.
4. **The two hyperplanes.** Take for `X` the hyperplane `V` of functions `Δ → ZMod l` with sum `0`,
   which has the fixed point `0`. The same set carries a second action: read `x` as `e + x` on the
   hyperplane of sum `1`, with `e` the indicator of `1 ∈ Δ`
   ([`hyperplaneAction`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L423)). This action is affine,
   and its linear part is the first one.
   [`apply_diagonalPermModule_eq_of_affine`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L518) shows
   that the two actions give the same value of `φ`. The two actions are sections of the projection
   from a group `H` of pairs (element of `G`, affine map of `V` with that linear part). They differ
   by translations, which form a normal subgroup killed by `l`
   ([`perm_pow_eq_one_of_map_sub`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L408)). Then dévissage
   ([`apply_restrictAlong_eq_of_inv_mul_mem`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L490))
   reduces to modules on which this subgroup acts trivially, where the two restrictions are
   isomorphic.
5. **Higman's property on the shifted hyperplane.** On the hyperplane of sum `1`, the translates of
   evaluation at `1` sum to `1` over `Δ`
   ([`sum_hyperplaneAction_toPerm_apply_one`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L453)).
   Since `l ∤ #S`, this gives a Higman map for the functions on it
   ([`isHigmanProjective_diagonalPermModule`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L278)). So
   `f(V × Δ ⧸ K) = 1` for every `K`: first curry, then pass from the linear action to the affine
   one, then use the hypothesis on Higman modules. Step 3 then gives `φ A = f(Δ ⧸ Δ) = 1`.

## The other tools in the file

- [`devissage_induction`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L154): a property that holds on
  modules killed by `l` and fixed by a normal `P`, whose elements have `l`-power powers in `Q`, and
  that is stable under extensions, holds for all modules killed by `l ^ j`. The induction is on
  length: the vectors fixed by `P` and killed by `l` form a nonzero submodule, by Cauchy's theorem
  applied to the `l`-group through which `P` acts.
- [`exists_open_sylow`](../src/Lib/General/Algebra/CartanMap.lean#L201): the preimage of a Sylow
  `l`-subgroup of `G ⧸ U`, an open subgroup of index prime to `l`.

## Where it is used

- **Exact cocycle counts.**
  [`card_trivialised_eq_of_map_range_eq_top`](../src/Lib/ClassField/GreenbergWiles/CocycleCounts.lean#L545)
  applies the theorem to the ratio `#(trivialised cocycles of X) / #X ^ (#ι + 1)`. The ratio is
  multiplicative when cocycles lift along short exact sequences. It is `1` on Higman modules by
  [`card_trivialisedCocycles_eq_of_isHigmanProjective`](../src/Lib/ClassField/GreenbergWiles/CocycleCounts.lean#L454).
  This feeds
  [`hasFewCocycles_of_central`](../src/Lib/ClassField/GreenbergWiles/CocycleCounts.lean#L685), which
  also uses `exists_open_sylow` and `devissage_induction`. The note `cocycle-counts.md` (PR #35140)
  explains the projective count.
- **Local duality.** `devissage_induction` is used in
  `Lib/ClassField/LocalDuality/DualityInequality.lean` (line 238), and `exists_open_sylow` in
  `Lib/ClassField/LocalDuality/CrystallineDuality.lean` (line 734). This note does not go through
  those uses.

## What the note does not claim

The comparison with the Cartan map is the file's own; this note does not check it against Serre's
statement. The theorem here is stated for modules with a central `z` and `ρ(z) − 1` injective, as in
the source.
